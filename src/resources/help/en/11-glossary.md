# Glossary

**Article**: a small piece of a file posted on Usenet. A post is made of thousands of articles.

**Newsgroup**: a Usenet forum where articles are posted, for example `alt.binaries.test`.

**NZB**: a file with the list of the articles of a post. Needed to download it.

**par2**: recovery files. They allow a downloader to repair a post when some articles are missing.

**Redundancy**: the percentage of extra par2 data. Higher means safer but bigger.

**Obfuscation**: hiding the names in the subjects of the articles. Only people with the NZB can find the post.

**RAR, 7z**: archive formats used to pack the files, with an optional password.

**Connection**: one link to the server. More connections upload faster, up to your provider limit.

**SSL**: encryption of the connection with the server.

**ETA**: estimated time left.

**Queue**: the list of posts waiting to be uploaded.

**Provider**: the company that gives you access to a Usenet server.
